# The local fifth-pivot supremum is sharp

In the rational box in the [pivot proof](../pivot/RESULTS.md), the retained family with \(-1\le r<1\) has supremum \(g_*\). Allowing \(r=1\) gives a matrix in the same box that attains \(g_*\).

For a fixed \(z\), let \((x_0(z),y_0(z))\) be the unique point in the box where both \(P=H_{11}+H_{21}\) and \(D=H_{22}-H_{11}\) vanish. The interval proof gives opposite endpoint signs for \(D\) along \(P=0\), together with \(P_y>0\) and \(D_x-D_yP_x/P_y>0\). Hence the point is interior in both \(x\) and \(y\). These inequalities make the Jacobian of \((D,P)\) with respect to \((x,y)\) invertible. The implicit function theorem and uniqueness give a continuous comparator curve on the closed \(z\)-interval and a continuously differentiable curve in its interior.

Set \(g(z)=2H_{11}\) on this curve. The exact interval bounds give \(4.13<g(z)<4.14\), and elimination gives \(E(g(z),z)=0\). The two endpoint polynomials \(E(g,z)\) at \(z=0.45322490\) and \(z=0.45322501\) each have one root in \((4.13,4.14)\), both below \(4.13251707863247283\). At the interior rational value \(z=0.45322491\) the unique root in that range is above the same cut. These are exact root counts with every interval endpoint checked to be nonzero. [Root comparison checker](pivot_attainment.py)

Thus \(g(z)\) attains its maximum at an interior point \(z_0\). There \(g'(z_0)=0\), so differentiating \(E(g(z),z)=0\) gives \(E_z(g(z_0),z_0)=0\). The leading coefficient of \(E\) in \(z\) is \((g-8)(g-6)(g-4)^2(g-2)(g^2-12g+40)\), which is nonzero throughout \((4.13,4.14)\). The multiple root in \(z\) therefore forces the discriminant to vanish. Its only root in \((4.13,4.14)\) is \(g_*\), so \(g(z_0)=g_*\). At the comparator all pivot comparisons hold, including the final equality \(r=1\). [Discriminant checker](../pivot/comparator.py)

At fixed \(z_0\), take points on \(P=0\) with \(x<x_0(z_0)\) tending to \(x_0(z_0)\). They remain in the box. The verified strict increase of \(D\) makes \(r<1\) there, and continuity makes \(r>-1\) sufficiently close to the comparator. The two retained off-diagonal ties and this ratio ensure stage-four dominance. The first three stages already satisfy their dominance bounds throughout the box. Their fifth pivots approach \(g_*\) from below because the fifth pivot is strictly increasing with \(x\) along \(P=0\). This gives sharpness over the stated open family.
